<p>OMA Concepts, doing business as PhoodScout ("<strong>PhoodScout</strong>," "<strong>we</strong>," "<strong>us</strong>," or "<strong>our</strong>"), operates the PhoodScout mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our Service. By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. This Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference into our <a href="/terms.html">Terms of Service</a>.</p>

<div class="section" id="collect">
  <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
  <h3>1.1 Information You Provide Directly</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Account Information:</strong> When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and password (stored in hashed form by Supabase).</li>
    <li><strong>Profile Information:</strong> Optionally, you may provide a home city, bio, social media handles, and a favorite food category.</li>
    <li><strong>Price Reports and Menu Data:</strong> Food prices, menu item names, categories, restaurant names, and city information you submit to the Service.</li>
    <li><strong>Menu Scanner Images:</strong> Photographs of physical menus submitted through the Menu Scanner feature. See Section 5 for full details.</li>
    <li><strong>Support Communications:</strong> If you contact us, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your communication.</li>
  </ul>
  <h3>1.2 Information Collected Automatically</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Location Data:</strong> With your explicit permission, we access your device's GPS location to center the map and identify nearby restaurants. We do not continuously track or store your location history. Location access is controlled by your device permissions and may be revoked at any time.</li>
    <li><strong>Device Information:</strong> We may collect your device type, operating system version, and app version for technical support and compatibility purposes.</li>
    <li><strong>Usage Data:</strong> We collect information about your interactions with the Service, such as features accessed, screens viewed, and actions taken, to understand usage patterns and improve the Service.</li>
  </ul>
  <h3>1.3 Device Identifiers</h3>
  <p>If you use our Android application, we may collect your Android device identifier (Android ID) for analytics and fraud prevention purposes. This is disclosed in compliance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements. This identifier is not used to track you across other apps or websites.</p>
</div>

    <li>Calculate and award Scout Points, Badges, and Scout Tier designations;</li>
    <li>Process menu images through our third-party AI service to extract menu item information;</li>
    <li>Personalize your experience, including displaying restaurants near your location;</li>
    <li>Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support;</li>
    <li>Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve Service functionality;</li>
    <li>Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity;</li>
    <li>Comply with applicable legal obligations; and</li>
    <li>Send you service-related communications (not marketing).</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="section" id="location">
  <h2>4. Location Data</h2>
  <p>We access your device location only with your explicit permission, granted through the operating system permission dialog. The following applies to all location data:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Location is used solely to center the map and surface nearby restaurants;</li>
    <li>Location access is controlled entirely by your device's operating system permission settings and may be denied or revoked at any time without affecting core Service functionality;</li>
    <li>We do not build a history of your physical locations or continuously track your movements; and</li>
    <li>Your home city, if provided in your profile, is stored as a text string and is not used for precise location determination or tracking.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="section" id="scanner">
  <h2>5. Menu Scanner Images</h2>
  <p>When you use the Menu Scanner feature, your submitted photograph is transmitted to OpenRouter (our AI service provider) for text extraction. The following applies to all Menu Scanner images:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Images are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections;</li>
    <li>OMA Concepts does not permanently store your image after AI processing is complete;</li>
    <li>OpenRouter's privacy policy governs their handling of image data during and after processing;</li>
    <li>Only the text and pricing data extracted from the image is retained as part of the community menu database; and</li>
    <li>Use of the Menu Scanner is voluntary and requires explicit consent before each first use.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

    <li><strong>Right to Correct:</strong> Request correction of inaccurate personal information.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing:</strong> We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out action is required, but you may submit a request confirming this.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:</strong> We do not use sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those permitted by law.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Non-Discrimination:</strong> We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>To exercise any of these rights, contact us at <a href="mailto:hello@omaconcepts.com">hello@omaconcepts.com</a> with the subject line "<strong>California Privacy Rights Request</strong>." We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within 45 days.</p>

<div class="section" id="gpc">

Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes, the practical impact is limited, but the signal is processed and honored as required by law.</p>
