What It Does

Know what you'll pay
before you go.

🏫

Real Prices

Prices submitted by real people who just ordered. Not scraped from a website. Not guessed from a photo. Actually verified by the community.

🌊

Map-first

Open the app and see every restaurant near you on a live map. Tap any pin to see the full menu with community-reported prices.

🧿

Menu Scanner

Point your camera at a physical menu. AI extracts every item and price in seconds. Review, edit, and submit to help everyone nearby.

✅

Price Confidence

A weighted consensus engine filters outliers and prioritizes recent reports. The price you see reflects what people are actually paying today.

🎉

Scout Rewards

Earn Scout Points for every verified price report. Level up from Scout to Verified Scout to Influencer. Badges, streaks, and leaderboards.

🚩

Food Trucks Too

Food trucks move. PhoodScout tracks them by city, shows where they've been spotted recently, and keeps their menus up to date.

Available on every platform.

iOS, Android, and web. The same live data everywhere. Use it in the car, at the counter, or before you leave home.
